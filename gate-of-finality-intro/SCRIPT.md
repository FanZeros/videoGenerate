## Letter (cell 15 only, one continuous page)

To my unseen child.
If you read this, I am probably gone.
Years ago I took my companions to the gate that promised answers.
The gate opened. Inside was not an answer, but a light that swallows all.
I leave you this hat. It is not glory. It is a warning.
If you ever hear the gate calling, do not go alone.
-- Father
